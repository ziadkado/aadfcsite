What's on

Events through the year

Two dates anchor our calendar — Taste of Africa each February and RoboTech each July — with movie nights, fundraisers and volunteering in between.

Our annual calendar

The dates we keep every year

Exact dates and ticket links for each edition are announced on our Facebook page and through Eventbrite.

February Annual Black History Month

Taste of Africa

Our flagship celebration at the Arden Theatre: African dance, drumming, storytelling, poetry and armchair travels, a fashion parade of traditional dress from ten-plus African countries, and a tasting of dishes from across Africa and the Caribbean.

Arden Theatre, 5 St. Anne Street $20 · Ticketmaster First come, first serve
Details
July Annual Summer camp

RoboTech Summer Camp

Five weekday mornings of hands-on robotics and technology for children aged 8–13, held over one week in July.

9:00 am – 12:00 noon $20 per camp Ages 8–13
Details

AADFC St. Albert, Alberta

The Africans & African Descendants Friendship Club of St. Albert is a community-based not-for-profit promoting and preserving African and Afro-Caribbean cultures and history, supporting members of the African-descent community, and creating ways to volunteer in St. Albert.
