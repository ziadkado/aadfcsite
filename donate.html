Where it goes

What your donation pays for

We are a volunteer-run club. Money given to us goes into programs, not overheads.

Taste of Africa

Theatre hire, performers, traditional food for hundreds of guests, and keeping tickets at $20 so that price is never a barrier.

Camps for children

Robotics kits and materials, so that a week of RoboTech can stay at $20 for every family in the club.

Welfare in the community

Groceries bought and delivered to neighbours in need, and transport for seniors getting to medical appointments.

Another way to help

Give what you no longer need

The Value Village FUNDrive® is an eco-friendly fundraiser for non-profit organisations. We collect gently-used items from around the community — clothes, household goods, books — and FUNDrive buys them from us by weight.

It costs you nothing but a clear-out, it keeps usable things out of landfill, and it turns directly into program funding. Our 2021 drive ran from 9 October to 20 November with a $1,000 goal.

Give your time instead

Hours are as valuable as dollars. Volunteers run our events, deliver our groceries and drive our neighbours to appointments.

AADFC St. Albert, Alberta

The Africans & African Descendants Friendship Club of St. Albert is a community-based not-for-profit promoting and preserving African and Afro-Caribbean cultures and history, supporting members of the African-descent community, and creating ways to volunteer in St. Albert.
