What we run

Programs for every part of the community

Our programs are built by members, for the community — cultural celebration, youth development, welfare and volunteering, all led by people who live here.

Flagship · Annual

Taste of Africa

Our Black History Month celebration at the Arden Theatre, held every February since 2020 — and bigger and better each year.

Taste of Africa is a variety show: African dance, drumming, storytelling, poetry and armchair travels, followed by a fashion parade of traditional dress from more than ten African countries, Nigeria, Rwanda, Uganda and Namibia among them. The evening ends with a tasting of dishes from across Africa and the Caribbean, packaged into to-go boxes for every guest.

It is the night the whole city is invited to — and the clearest expression of what the club is for.

It Takes A Village To Raise A Child

The theme that guides our youth work. Young people grow up steadier when the adults around them are many, visible and invested — so we make sure they are.

Led by Toni Harrison, Director of Youth Mentorship & Community Engagement.

Youth

Youth mentorship

Connecting young people in St. Albert with mentors from their own community.

Our youth mentorship events bring together children, teenagers and the adults who can guide them — people who share their heritage and have already walked the road they are on. It is about belonging as much as advice: seeing your culture reflected in the people who succeed around you.

Ages 8–13

RoboTech summer camp

A week of hands-on robotics and technology, priced so that cost is never the reason a child misses out.

Children spend their mornings building, programming and problem-solving together. The camp runs in July over five weekday mornings, and at $20 for the week it is deliberately within reach of every family in the club.

Film, food, friendship and fun

Our screenings have included award-winning filmmaker Byron Hurt's documentary on soul food — its cultural significance in Black identity, its enduring popularity, and the economic and social factors that limit food choices in Black communities.

Snacks and a discussion led by our Vice President, Toni Harrison, follow every film.

Community

Movie nights

An evening of film and conversation, hosted with our partners around the city.

We choose films that give the room something to talk about — stories about identity, food, history and justice — then stay behind afterwards to talk about them properly, over snacks.

Welfare

Community service & welfare

The quiet work: looking after the wellbeing of our community and the wider city, week in and week out.

Groceries, bought and delivered

We purchase and deliver groceries to help ensure that people in need have access to food.

Edmonton Food Bank shifts

Members volunteer at the Edmonton Food Bank, adding our hands to a service the whole region depends on.

Rides for seniors

We provide seniors with transportation to their medical appointments, so that getting there is never the obstacle.

Settlement

Newcomer support

Arriving somewhere new is easier when someone who has done it is already waiting for you.

We keep resources for people new to St. Albert and connect them with the club, so that a first winter here comes with company. Our members also volunteer their time in support of local organisations providing immigrant settlement services — including St. Albert Further Education, which offers training and helps newcomers integrate into the community.

Fundraising

Our fundraisers keep the programs running. The Value Village FUNDrive® is an eco-friendly fundraiser for non-profits: we collect gently-used items from around the community, and FUNDrive buys them from us by weight.

Our 2021 drive ran from 9 October to 20 November with a $1,000 goal.

AADFC St. Albert, Alberta

The Africans & African Descendants Friendship Club of St. Albert is a community-based not-for-profit promoting and preserving African and Afro-Caribbean cultures and history, supporting members of the African-descent community, and creating ways to volunteer in St. Albert.
