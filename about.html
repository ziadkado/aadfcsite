Who we are

A friendship club built by neighbours, for neighbours

The Africans & African Descendants Friendship Club of St. Albert is a community-based not-for-profit. We promote and preserve African and Afro-Caribbean cultures and history, support members of the African-descent community, and find ways for our members to volunteer across St. Albert.

Our story

It started with a need, and an evening in the park

The club was founded in 2019 by Helen Agbonison, a Nigerian expatriate who saw that people of African descent living in St. Albert had no natural place to come together. There was a clear need for a group of this nature in the community — so she started one.

Our first gathering was on 25 August 2019, tagged An Evening in the Park. Since then the club has hosted a steady run of cultural and volunteering events across the city, and grown into a recognised part of St. Albert's community life.

Today we are a place where newcomers find their footing, where children see their heritage celebrated on a real stage, and where anyone who wants to help their neighbours can find something useful to do.

  1. 2019

    The club is founded

    Helen Agbonison brings together people of African descent living in St. Albert. The first event, An Evening in the Park, is held on 25 August.

  2. February 2020

    The first Taste of Africa

    Our Black History Month celebration launches — and becomes the event the club is now known for across the city.

  3. 2021

    Fundraising and welfare grow

    The Value Village FUNDrive® drive runs through the autumn, alongside grocery deliveries, food bank shifts and rides for seniors.

  4. Today

    A programme for every age

    Youth mentorship, robotics camps, movie nights and newcomer support sit alongside our annual celebration at the Arden Theatre.

Our mission

  • To raise awareness of African cultures and history to St. Albert residents from Africans & African Descendants living in St. Albert, Alberta, through events and programs.
  • To build connections within the African & African Descendants community in St. Albert for friendship and support.
  • To organise our membership to give back to the greater St. Albert community.

Our vision

To continue to create a rich and vibrant African and African Descendant community in the City of St. Albert.

We wish to leave a legacy for our children and for future generations of Africans and African descendants — a community that is already here waiting for them, with its doors open.

Recognition

Recognised for building an inclusive community

Our members volunteer well beyond our own events. We buy and deliver groceries for neighbours who need them, take shifts at the Edmonton Food Bank, and drive seniors to their medical appointments.

Club members also give their time to other local organisations providing settlement services for immigrants — among them St. Albert Further Education, which offers training and helps newcomers integrate into the community.
