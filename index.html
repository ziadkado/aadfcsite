Our mission

Three commitments that guide everything we do

Share our cultures

To raise awareness of African cultures and history among St. Albert residents — through events and programs led by the Africans and African Descendants who live here.

Build connections

To build connections within the African and African Descendant community in St. Albert, so that friendship and support are never more than a phone call away.

Give back

To organise our membership to give back to the greater St. Albert community — volunteering, mobilising, and showing up wherever we are needed.

Our flagship event

Taste of Africa

Every February since 2020, we take over the Arden Theatre to celebrate Black History Month with the city — and it has grown bigger and better every year since.

The evening is a variety show in the fullest sense: African dance, drumming, storytelling, poetry and armchair travels, followed by a fashion parade of traditional dress from more than ten African countries — Nigeria, Rwanda, Uganda and Namibia among them. It closes the way every good gathering should, with food: a tasting of dishes from across Africa and the Caribbean, packed into to-go boxes for every guest.

City of St. Albert, Alberta

Rooted here

Everything we do happens inside these lines

The club is not a national organisation with a branch in St. Albert. It is St. Albert: our celebrations, our camps, our grocery runs and our volunteering all happen within the city limits.

Three of our five partners share a single address. St. Albert Place, at 5 St. Anne Street, was designed by Douglas Cardinal and opened in 1984, its curves following the bend of the Sturgeon River. The Arden Theatre, the St. Albert Public Library and the Musée Héritage Museum are all inside it — which means the building that hosts Taste of Africa each February is the same one that holds the city's library and its museum.

AADFC St. Albert, Alberta

The Africans & African Descendants Friendship Club of St. Albert is a community-based not-for-profit promoting and preserving African and Afro-Caribbean cultures and history, supporting members of the African-descent community, and creating ways to volunteer in St. Albert.
